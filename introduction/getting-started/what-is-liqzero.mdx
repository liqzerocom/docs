LiqZero is trading infrastructure for teams that want to build their own perpetual futures experience on top of shared liquidity. Instead of constructing a matching engine, liquidity network, and settlement stack from scratch, you integrate LiqZero's APIs, SDKs, and smart contracts to launch your own product. LiqZero is not a consumer-facing exchange — it is a headless trading backend that sits underneath partner applications and powers the account, trading, market data, and settlement flows those applications expose to their users. The model is straightforward: your team owns the product, frontend, branding, and user relationship, while LiqZero provides the trading infrastructure underneath.

LiqZero is designed for teams building products that need professional-grade trading infrastructure without operating the full stack themselves.

## Why Teams Build on LiqZero

Building a perpetual futures venue from scratch requires solving two extraordinarily hard problems simultaneously: bootstrapping liquidity and building and maintaining the infrastructure. LiqZero removes both.

- **You keep the frontend and user relationship.** Your team controls the product experience while LiqZero handles the matching engine, orderbook, and settlement logic underneath.
- **You connect to shared liquidity instead of starting from zero.** Builders plug into a common trading backend and shared orderbook rather than trying to bootstrap isolated liquidity market by market.
- **You get CEX-like performance with on-chain settlement.** LiqZero combines low-latency trading infrastructure with fully documented on-chain deposit, withdrawal, and settlement flows that preserve user self-custody.
- **You get a builder-friendly business model.** Partners define their own end-user fee structure on top of LiqZero's base fee model, enabling custom monetization without operating a separate fee backend.

## What LiqZero Provides

LiqZero's primary product is omnichain trading infrastructure for perpetual futures. In practical terms, that means your integration gets access to:

- **Shared liquidity and orderbook access** — connect to the same liquidity layer used across the LiqZero ecosystem from day one
- **Account, order, and market data APIs** — REST and WebSocket interfaces covering registration, key management, order placement and cancellation, positions, funding rates, and live market data
- **Smart contract rails** — audited on-chain contracts for deposit, withdrawal, and settlement across all supported chains
- **Partner-facing controls** — fee configuration, referral management, builder ID, and operational workflow tooling

<Note>
  LiqZero's symbol format follows the pattern `PERP_<TOKEN>_USDC`. For example, ETH perpetuals are quoted as `PERP_ETH_USDC`. All market and order endpoints use this format.
</Note>

## Why Omnichain Matters

LiqZero's architecture is designed so partner applications can serve users across supported chains while all participants connect into the same trading system and shared orderbook. Users on Arbitrum, Base, Optimism, Solana, or any other supported chain interact with your application without switching chains — LiqZero's Asset Layer handles the per-chain deposit and withdrawal mechanics so your users see a seamless experience.

This design reduces the need to fragment liquidity by chain and makes it possible to deliver a consistent trading experience from a single partner integration. The full list of supported chains includes:
