LiqZero deploys its Asset Layer smart contracts on every supported chain, so users can register accounts, deposit USDC, trade perpetual futures, and withdraw back to their wallet — all without leaving the chain they already use. As a builder, you choose which of these chains to surface in your product. New chains are added over time as LiqZero expands the Asset Layer footprint.

## What "Supported" Means

A chain is supported when LiqZero has deployed its vault and account registration contracts on it and wired up the cross-chain relay to the Settlement Layer. On a supported chain, your users can:

- **Register** a LiqZero trading account linked to their wallet address
- **Deposit** USDC into the shared liquidity pool
- **Trade** perpetual futures against the shared orderbook
- **Withdraw** USDC back to their wallet on any supported chain

No bridging step is required at any point. The user's funds flow in and out of LiqZero entirely through the Asset Layer contracts on their native chain.

## Chain Coverage for Builders

When you build on LiqZero, you decide which supported chains to expose to your users. A user arriving with a Base wallet can deposit and trade without you needing to support every chain in the list. You can launch with a subset of chains and expand later — adding a new chain means pointing your frontend at the correct Asset Layer contract address for that chain. No changes to your Engine Layer integration are required.

<Tip>
  Start with the chains your target users are most active on. Arbitrum, Base, and Optimism cover the majority of EVM DeFi activity and have well-established USDC liquidity. Solana is the recommended starting point for non-EVM coverage.
</Tip>

## Chain Additions

LiqZero adds new chains on an ongoing basis. When a new chain launches, the Asset Layer contracts are deployed, the cross-chain relay is configured, and the chain becomes available for builders to integrate. Watch the product changelog or join the builder community for announcements about new chain support.

<Note>
  The chain list above reflects currently supported mainnet deployments. Testnet support may differ. Check the [Contract Addresses](/api-reference/contracts/addresses) page for the most current deployment status of each chain, including testnet contract addresses where available.
</Note>
