OmniVault is LiqZero's flagship strategy vault — a permissionless, omnichain yield product that lets any user earn passive returns by contributing USDC liquidity to professional market-making strategies. Unlike most yield products that require a specific chain or wrapping step, OmniVault accepts deposits from any supported chain in a single transaction. The vault is managed by Kronos Research as the inaugural Strategy Provider, and earnings flow back proportionally to every depositor based on their share of the pool.

## What OmniVault Is

OmniVault is a smart-contract-based strategy vault that sits at the intersection of LiqZero's shared liquidity layer and institutional market-making. It is:

- **Permissionless** — any wallet can deposit; there is no whitelist or minimum beyond the on-chain transaction cost
- **Omnichain** — deposits are accepted from any LiqZero-supported chain through the Asset Layer
- **Yield-generating** — deposited USDC is actively deployed into market-making strategies by the Strategy Provider
- **Proportional** — each depositor earns a share of OmniVault's PnL proportional to their contribution

OmniVault opens up profit opportunities that were previously inaccessible to retail participants — the kind of returns that traditionally required a seat at an institutional market-making desk.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Deposit USDC from any supported chain">
    You call the OmniVault smart contract on your chain (Arbitrum, Base, Optimism, or SEI) and deposit USDC. The contract records your share of the vault and forwards the assets to the strategy pool. No bridging is required before you deposit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Strategy Provider manages the assets">
    Kronos Research — LiqZero's Strategy Provider — deploys the pooled USDC into market-making strategies on the LiqZero orderbook. These strategies provide liquidity to the shared orderbook and capture spread and funding rate returns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="PnL accrues to the vault">
    As the market-making strategies generate returns (or losses), the vault's net asset value changes. Your proportional share of the vault reflects these changes in real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Withdraw USDC at any time">
    You can request a withdrawal from the OmniVault at any time. Your USDC plus your proportional share of accumulated PnL is released back to your wallet through the Asset Layer contract on your chain.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Strategy Provider: Kronos Research

Kronos Research is LiqZero's inaugural Strategy Provider (SP) for OmniVault. As an SP, Kronos Research operates at arm's length from LiqZero and manages OmniVault assets independently. Kronos Research's market-making strategies provide bid-ask liquidity on the LiqZero orderbook, improving price discovery and reducing slippage for all traders in the ecosystem — while generating returns for OmniVault depositors.

<Note>
  Strategy Providers are independent third parties. LiqZero does not guarantee the performance of any Strategy Provider and does not assume liability for their trading decisions. All yields generated by OmniVault are subject to market conditions and may fluctuate. Past performance is not indicative of future results.
</Note>

## Security

LiqZero has completed two independent security audits on the OmniVault smart contracts. Both audit reports are publicly available in the [LiqZero Audits GitHub repository](https://github.com/OrderlyNetwork/Audits). The audits cover the vault contract logic, deposit and withdrawal flows, and access control mechanisms.

<Warning>
  Every strategy vault carries inherent risks, including market risk, smart contract risk, and counterparty risk from the Strategy Provider. Carefully assess your risk tolerance and review the audit reports before depositing. The value of your deposited USDC can decrease if the strategy generates losses.
</Warning>

## Depositing into OmniVault

To deposit into OmniVault, connect your wallet on any of the four supported chains (Arbitrum, Base, Optimism, or SEI) and call the vault contract directly or through the LiqZero interface. The deposit flow is the same as a standard Asset Layer deposit — the vault contract handles the share accounting automatically.

<CodeGroup>
```solidity Solidity — Deposit Call
// Approve the vault contract to spend your USDC first
IERC20(USDC_ADDRESS).approve(OMNIVAULT_ADDRESS, depositAmount);

// Deposit USDC into OmniVault
IOmniVault(OMNIVAULT_ADDRESS).deposit(depositAmount);
```

```typescript TypeScript — Using the SDK
import { OmniVault } from '@liqzero/sdk';

const vault = new OmniVault({
  chainId: 42161, // Arbitrum
  signer: yourEthersSigner,
});

// Deposit 1000 USDC (6 decimal places)
const tx = await vault.deposit('1000000000');
await tx.wait();
console.log('Deposited successfully:', tx.hash);
```
</CodeGroup>
